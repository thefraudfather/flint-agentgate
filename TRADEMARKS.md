# FLINT trademarks and verification marks

The MIT license applies to the source code in this repository. It does not grant permission to use FLINT trademarks, logos, product names, trade dress, or verification marks in a way that suggests sponsorship, endorsement, certification, or affiliation.

Forks may accurately state that they are based on this repository. They must not:

- describe a locally or independently issued credential as FLINT verified;
- display the FLINT Stamp or a confusingly similar verification mark;
- represent that FLINT assessed, monitors, endorses, or operates the fork; or
- use FLINT branding as the primary identity of a redistributed product without written permission.

Community credentials generated by this repository are self-attested. Authoritative FLINT verification requires a credential signed by a FLINT-controlled issuer and validated against a FLINT trust anchor.

Questions about permitted brand use can be directed through https://flint.network/.
